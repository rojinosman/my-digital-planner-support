My Digital Planner

Support & help for the iPad digital planner app.

What this app does

My Digital Planner is an iPad handwriting planner with month and week pages. Write with Apple Pencil, flip between pages, zoom and pan like a paper notebook. Notes stay on your device.

Getting started

  • Open the app to jump to the current week.
  • Use the month and week controls at the top to move around your planner.
  • Swipe with your finger to turn pages; use Apple Pencil to write.
  • Pinch to zoom; when zoomed in, drag with one finger to pan.
