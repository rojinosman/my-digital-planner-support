Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

My Digital Planner (“the App”) is provided by Rojin Osman. This policy explains how the App handles information.

Information we collect

The App does not collect, transmit, or sell personal data. Planner pages and handwriting you create are stored on your device using on-device storage. We do not operate analytics, advertising, or account systems inside the App.

Data storage

Your notes remain on your iPad unless you choose to share or back them up through your own device features (for example, device backups).

Children’s privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
